# Food & Drink (`foodie`)

## Persona
Cares who is cooking and where the mezcal comes from. Supper clubs, pop-ups, tastings, markets with real producers.

## Would never feature
A generic restaurant promo, a chain's "taco night", a brunch special with no reason to exist.
- A running special menu, chain or not (Madero Restaurante seasonal menu; Fónico cocineras dinners → None). DP: Fónico read as a special menu over several dates, rare but not a one-off night.

## Charter
- The bar is "would this curator feature it," not "is it in the category." Category match is necessary, not sufficient.
- The test is scarcity, not who's cooking: a one-night dinner (Taberna del León, Oct 29) or a bounded festival (Cacao para todos) is an event; a special menu you can book any of several nights is not (Fónico, Madero). Confirmed by DP, session 1.
- Named cook/producer still matters for quality (Patiño, Nawal Cacao), but it didn't decide anything in session 1.
- A producers' fair qualifies when the product is food or drink (cacao, chocolate, pozol, tejate); a flower growers' fair did not (Festival Flores de Cempasúchil → None).
- Price is not a filter: $2,250/person was featured.
- A chain or multi-location promo is out even in season.
