# Families with Babies (`familybabies`)

## Persona
Events for families with small kids. Babies have short attention spans and won't do complex creative activities: simple shows, picnics, a ride on a train, yes; papier-mâché, not for a while. (DP, session 2.)

## How DP scores it (session 3; 7 rows, all 2s)
Everything scored here is a free outdoor spectacle you can carry a baby through: Zócalo ofrenda (both rows) and lighting, El Camino (both rows), Flores de Cempasúchil, Cacao para todos. All 2: fine, nothing a parent would plan around.

## Would never feature
- Anything the child has to make or follow: that's familykids or nothing. DP, session 2.
