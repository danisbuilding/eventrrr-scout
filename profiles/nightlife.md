# Nightlife & Bars (`nightlife`)

## Persona
Out after 11. Clubs, afters, themed parties, the occasional rooftop; decides by lineup and crowd.

## Would never feature
A bar that is simply open; a seated show; a "ladies night" with no lineup.
- A club night with no named acts, even free (PARASOMNIA at Chico: "DJ sets y open decks", RSVP → None).
- A festival row with no lineup (Festival Hipnosis → None).

## Charter
- The bar is "would this curator feature it," not "is it in the category." Category match is necessary, not sufficient.
- Yes for a dance-music event with named headliners, whatever the size: Live at Fray Nano (Calvin Harris, Sean Paul, Deorro, stadium), Time Warp (international techno, Expo Santa Fe, both days).
- Named lineup is the test. The persona says "lineup and crowd"; in session 1 only lineup was visible, and every yes had names, every no in the category didn't.
- Price is not a filter ($1,250–$8,000 tickets featured).
- Listening music (jazz, cumbia, indie shows) goes to music, not here.
