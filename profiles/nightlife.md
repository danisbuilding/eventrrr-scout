# Nightlife & Bars (`nightlife`)

## Persona
Out after 11. Clubs, afters, themed parties, the occasional rooftop; decides by lineup and crowd.

## How DP scores it (session 3; 7 scored rows, nothing above 3)
The sources barely cover clubs, so this calendar's top is a 3 so far. A reader can walk into any 3 cold and have a night; the 2s are big ticketed festivals; the 1 is a small show nobody would send a stranger to.

- **3 — good.** Broad-appeal dance nights at any size or price: Live at Fray Nano (Calvin Harris, Sean Paul); Brown Sugar: Rewind (Y2K hip hop / R&B, Revo Rooftop, $150); Jazz en las Rocas (free, 4pm; see Surprises).
- **2 — fine.** Festivals that read as concerts first: Time Warp (both days), Festival Hipnosis.
- **1 — mediocre.** Mary in the Junkyard at Foro Indie Rocks (unknown act, uncomfortable room).
- **No row.** PARASOMNIA at Chico (niche sounds; it's a music 2 now, not nightlife), Fenómeno Fuzz (moved to music).

## Would never feature
- A bar that is simply open; a seated show; a "ladies night" with no lineup.
- Intense niche sounds you won't groove to unless you already love them (tribal, jungle, ghettotech): no nightlife row. DP, session 1.
