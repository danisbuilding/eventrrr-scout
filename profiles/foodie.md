# Food & Drink (`foodie`)

## Persona
Cares who is cooking and where the mezcal comes from. Supper clubs, pop-ups, tastings, markets with real producers.

## How DP scores it (session 3; 7 scored rows, 2 suspected mis-taps)
Producers and traditional cooks outrank chef dinners. The expensive one-night restaurant dinners that were the featured picks in sessions 1–2 are 2s now; the mezcal-and-producers ofrenda and the cocineras dinners are 3s.

- **1 — mediocre.** A running seasonal menu (Madero Restaurante).
